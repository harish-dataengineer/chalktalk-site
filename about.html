Why "explain it simply" matters to me

In the real industry, the engineers who explain complex systems in plain language are the ones people trust and want to work with. That's the same instinct behind every lesson here — the plain-English version always comes first, the textbook definition comes right after, so it actually sticks instead of just being memorized for one exam.

If ChalkTalk helps even one student stop dreading a subject they used to fear, that's the whole point of building this.
